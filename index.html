Where did I get these results from / Methodology?

These are informal predictions done by someone who follows politics as a hobby, and as such take any predictions with a grain of salt. Most of my predictions are a combination of current polling, fundamentals, and my personal opinion. Here, I will explain what each of these mean and how they come into play.

Polling - Arguably the simplest one. I heavily weigh in polling into my predictions, as it gives a good general indicator of candidate quality. For example, polling consistently shows James Talarico as a strong candidate against Ken Paxton, who consistently polls as a weak candidate. Or alternatively, we can apply this to states like Nebraska, where polling suggests Dan Osborn is running a significantly weaker campaign this year compared to two years ago.

Fundamentals - Looking at a state, it's demographics, and it's national environment. Polling and early vote shows significant swings in Hispanic voters to the Democratic Party, and is most demonstrated in Mexican-Americans. As such, I weigh in these fundamentals to states with large Hispanic populations, such as Texas, Florida, and Georgia. Additionally, the national environment suggest that Democrats are now the party of high propensity voters - highly informed voters that tend to vote even in little-known elections. Early voting has also shown weaker Republican turnout nationally.

My personal opinion - Of course, some of these are just in part my opinion. Some states I give heavier preference to either Democrats or Republicans, usually due to my personal opinion on how well a candidate is running their campaign.
